# AfD Discourse Explorer · 2013–2025

A compact digital research prototype for searching and comparing six official programme documents of the Alternative für Deutschland (AfD): 2013, 2014, 2016, 2017, 2021 and 2025.

The site supports multilingual concept search, year/topic filters, three analytical dimensions (membership, representation, final political authority), cross-year comparison, expandable context, auxiliary Chinese translation, PDF/page traceability, CSV export and citation generation.

This project accompanies a course paper on the evolution of AfD identity frames around “the German people” from 2013 to 2025. Automated tags and search results are navigation aids only; interpretation remains grounded in the original programme texts and their context.

Open the public site through GitHub Pages once Pages is enabled for the `main` branch root.
